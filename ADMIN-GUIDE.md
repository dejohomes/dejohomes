# Dejohomes editor quick guide

Open `https://YOUR_DOMAIN/studio` and sign in with your invited Sanity account. You can also open the Studio during local development at `http://localhost:3000/studio`.

## Add a property

1. Choose **Property listings** in the left sidebar, then select **Create new**.
2. Enter a clear listing name. Open **Page link** and select **Generate** to create its web address.
3. Fill in the estate, exact Abuja location, current advertised price in naira, previous price (if applicable), property size in square metres, home type, title/document type, description and availability. The prototype starts with the ten offers transcribed from the supplied Dejohomes flyers; verify prices and availability with the team before publishing updates.
4. Add payment plan information, amenities and directions. Upload approved property renders or photos under **Photos**. Drag photos to change their display order.
5. Turn on **Show on the homepage** to feature the property. Preview the draft in Studio, then publish when the details are ready.

To update availability, open the listing and change **Availability** to Available, Few available, or Sold, then publish. To temporarily remove it from the public site, unpublish it. To permanently remove it, use the document menu and choose Delete. Duplicate a listing from that same menu, then update its details before publishing.

## Update contact and homepage details

Open **Site settings** to update the home headline and introduction, company description, phone, WhatsApp number (digits only, including country code), email and office address. Use a full international WhatsApp number without `+` or spaces, for example `2348012345678`. Publish changes when done.

## Manage enquiries

Open **Lead inbox** in the Studio top navigation to view messages. Search by name, phone or message, filter by follow-up status, mark a lead handled, and export the current view to CSV. Enquiries are stored only after the site owner configures the private Sanity write token. Email delivery also requires the owner to configure Resend; contact the site administrator if either service is unavailable.

## Editorial content

Use **Blog posts**, **Testimonials**, **FAQs**, **Team members** and **Project gallery** in Studio to maintain those records. The journal displays published blog posts. Check the public site after publishing to confirm the result.

If a published change does not appear within a minute, contact the site administrator to check the Sanity webhook and Vercel deployment configuration.
